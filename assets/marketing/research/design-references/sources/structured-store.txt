      - paragraph: "I love the features of this planner. It is the closest to how I set up my bullet journal. Being able to replan tasks (and decide whether they are valuable anymore) or pop them to an inbox so I can clear them out of my metal clutter but not lose them entirely are excellent features. I love the idea of “energy expenditure” as it forces me to really consider whether I will have the energy for something, not just the time. If I run out of steam before finishing all my duties in a day, I can make a more realistic plan in the future, given energy constraints, rather than kicking myself for being “lazy.” So important for mental health AND for getting the work done. The UI is excellent.I enter things into my Apple Calendar and then use Structured to check things off through the day, so I have noticed two items which might improve this further: I wish it could pull travel time from my Calendar, so that I don’t add tasks into space where I need to be driving; and it would be nice to be able to add an unexpected event or task (let’s say run to the grocery store for 30 mins) and have everything bump forward accordingly, rather than having to hand slide it to eliminate overlaps. If this were added, having some events able to be toggled as “locked” and un-bumpable would be important (for example, a class that happens at a certain time).Otherwise, really outstanding planner. Thoughtful updates and amazing attention to details that work well for ADHD brains!"
